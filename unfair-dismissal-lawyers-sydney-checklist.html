Matching Your Issue to the Right Category

The initial step in comparing unfair dismissal lawyers Sydney involves understanding the category that best fits your employment concern. The available categories typically separate matters related to unfair dismissal, employment contracts, and workplace investigations. This distinction is crucial because a single dispute might touch upon multiple areas of employment law.

If your primary concern is the termination of your employment, the unfair dismissal category is the most relevant starting point. However, if the dispute centres on the interpretation of your employment contract or the fairness of an internal investigation process, it is beneficial to also consider the contract law or workplace investigation categories. This does not necessitate contacting lawyers in all three streams; rather, it means clearly articulating the core of your problem in your initial communication. This allows the potential legal representative to quickly ascertain whether their expertise aligns with your specific needs or if another specialisation is more appropriate.

Reviews can serve as an initial prompt, but the true test of suitability lies in direct communication. Where pricing information is displayed, compare it diligently, as the platform encourages comparison of services and costs, not merely the names of the firms.
